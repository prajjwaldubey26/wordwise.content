# NxFlowAI — Global Jewellery Opportunity Batch

**Research date:** 30 July 2026  
**Scope:** Ten public-data prospects across India, the GCC, UK, US, and Australia.  
**Qualification bar:** A public LinkedIn company-size band of 51–200 employees (or a public headcount within that band), plus a recent expansion, funding, hiring, international-growth, or operational-growth signal.

## How to read this dossier

- **Verified** items are linked to a public source. **Hypotheses to validate** are operational implications, not claims that a company has a broken process.
- Scores are NxFlowAI prioritisation judgments, not claims about the company’s actual intention to buy. `Likelihood to buy` means likelihood of being receptive to a relevant operational-improvement conversation in the next 3–6 months.
- Private-company revenue is deliberately marked **not publicly disclosed** unless a public, attributable operating figure is available. This avoids invented financial data.
- **ROI** is an evidence-bound measurement plan rather than a fabricated currency number. The first discovery step should calculate: `(manual minutes removed × monthly volume × fully loaded hourly cost) + recovered contribution margin from faster conversion / fewer lost leads`.
- Website observations describe exposed journeys and hand-offs; they are not a security or performance audit.

## Scoring method

Each score is 1–100. Overall Opportunity = 25% Buying Intent + 15% Growth + 20% Automation Potential + 15% Budget + 10% Urgency + 5% each for Digital Maturity, Operational Complexity, and Decision-Maker Accessibility. Likelihood to Buy = 40% Buying Intent + 25% Urgency + 20% Budget + 15% Decision-Maker Accessibility.

## Recommended first outreach sequence

1. **Start with Cullen, Aukera, Jewelbox, and FoundRae.** Each has a recent, externally visible expansion event and a clear human-in-the-loop operational workflow.
2. Ask for a 20-minute workflow-mapping call, not an “AI demo.” Lead with the exact operational hand-off in that company’s profile.
3. Offer a narrowly scoped pilot: one service journey, two locations or one department, four weeks, dashboarded baseline and result metrics.
4. Do not claim that any company lacks a CRM, uses Excel, suffers lead leakage, or is actively shopping for AI. Those must be discovered in the call.

## Discovery questions that turn hypotheses into a quantified business case

- Which lead sources reach staff today, and what is the median first response time by source / store?
- How many hours per week go into manually creating quotes, briefs, appointment notes, service updates, or product information?
- Where is customer, order, stock, and custom-design information re-entered between systems?
- Which step most often delays a custom / repair / exchange / personalisation journey?
- Which business metric does leadership lack in a usable daily dashboard?
